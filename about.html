About Chelsea

Mobile massage therapy with flexibility built in.

Take Care Clinical Massage was created around a simple idea: therapy should be easier to access, especially for clients who prefer care where they already are.

I graduated from Makami College in 2016 and began my career in a physiotherapy clinic. That experience showed me how much clients appreciated having treatment come to them, and it inspired me to build an independent mobile practice.

For the past 5 years, I have loved the flexibility that mobile massage brings to my clients and to my work. My goal is to make treatment feel professional, calm, and convenient from the first message to the end of your appointment.
